# Local SEO master-template guide

Use `_templates/location-course-template.html` only as a source template. Do not publish pages until the placeholders are replaced with **genuinely useful and locally accurate** content.

## Required placeholders
- `{{COURSE_NAME}}`
- `{{CITY}}`
- `{{CANONICAL_URL}}`
- Module/project details specific to the course

## Recommended title formula
`{{COURSE_NAME}} Training in {{CITY}} | Aslam Khan Academy`

## Recommended meta description formula
`Explore {{COURSE_NAME}} training in {{CITY}}: syllabus, practical projects, prerequisites, current batch questions and career support.`

## Page hierarchy
1. H1: course + city
2. H2: learner intent / who it is for
3. H2: skills and syllabus
4. H2: practical projects
5. H2: batch / fees / learning mode
6. H2: verified location and directions
7. H2: local FAQs

## Structured data
Use `Course`, `BreadcrumbList`, and `FAQPage` where the visible page supports it. Add `LocalBusiness` only when a real, verified physical business location is being represented.

## Content quality rules
- Do not create separate near-duplicate pages for every keyword variation.
- Do not fabricate addresses, Google Maps pins, testimonials, student outcomes, fees, salary claims or placement percentages.
- Write as much content as needed to satisfy the search intent; do not pad pages to hit arbitrary character counts.
- Use course-specific and local information that a visitor can actually act on.

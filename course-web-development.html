Who it is for

A structured path for learners who want to build.

  • Students starting web development
  • Career switchers who want frontend foundations
  • Business owners or creators who want to understand modern websites
BY THE END, YOU SHOULD BE ABLE TO
  • Structure semantic, accessible web pages

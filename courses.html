Course selection

Four questions to ask before choosing.

01

What do you want to build?

Websites, applications, dashboards, automation or AI workflows require different foundations.

02

What is your current level?

Choose a program that starts where you actually are—not where a brochure assumes you are.

03

How much practice is included?

Ask about assignments, projects, review, missed-class support and independent work.

04

What evidence will you finish with?

Strong learning should leave you with explainable projects, documented work and clearer technical confidence.

Need help comparing?

Tell us your goal and current level.
